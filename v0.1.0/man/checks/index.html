Checking the phase

Two checks to run before reading anything into the phases: does the phase follow the value, and how long are the cycles.

Does the phase follow the value?

Tierbrief

PhaseValueCheck regresses each agent-frame's transformed value, z-scored within its track, on cos φ and sin φ.

Tierfull

r2 is the share of the value's variance explained by the phase alone; the rest is fluctuation outside the band, mostly bursts. peak is the phase at which the fitted value is highest. For a smooth oscillation it sits at 0, where the band-passed signal peaks. A pulse or a signal with a broad plateau and a sharp trough still has a clear relation to the phase, but peak can move off 0 and the binned medians (centers, median, q25, q75) show the shape.

In the motivating cortex model, the phase explained 44% of notch and NICD variance and 63–71% of the pulse-like Delta and Ngn.

PhaseValueCheck(p)
PhaseValueCheck: 22800 agent-frames; phase explains 71.3% of the variance; peak at φ = -0.1°

Cycle lengths

Tierbrief

A cycle ends each time φ crosses 0 going upward; CycleLengths returns the time between successive crossings, per agent.

Tierfull

Crossing times are interpolated linearly between frames, so cycle lengths are not quantised to the frame interval. Crossings are counted only within unbroken stretches of phase, so a masked frame never merges two cycles into one. A distribution centred inside the band with room on both sides is the sign that the band brackets the rhythm; one piled against a band edge means the band is cutting it.

c = CycleLengths(p)
CycleLengths: 1121 cycles from 200 agents, median 170.4
Tierdev

A step from φ = −3 to φ = +3 is a wrap, not an upward crossing of 0; crossings require b − a < π.

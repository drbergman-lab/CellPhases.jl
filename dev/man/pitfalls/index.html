Pitfalls

Mistakes that were made once while developing this analysis, and what guards against them.

Tierbrief

Each item names the mistake, what it did, and the guard.

The filter's sampling rate.

Tierfull

Without the sampling rate, DSP.jl reads cutoffs as fractions of the Nyquist frequency. A prototype filter passed 200–600 minutes instead of 100–300, suppressed the real rhythm, and produced a spurious "upward wave". Pure-sine tests missed it, because a single frequency keeps its phase when attenuated. bandpassFilter takes the band as periods in the unit of dt and always passes the sampling rate; the tests pin the half-power points and use a pulse train.

Phase wrapping at large radius.

Tierfull

Neighbourhoods wider than about half a wavelength wrap phase differences past ±π, and the gradient fit fails. Slow waves have short wavelengths. Compare two radii, and watch the share of clean fits. See Phase waves.

Pooling hides directions.

Tierfull

A pooled space-time correlation map averages opposite local waves to zero drift. Local phase gradients see them; a pooled map does not.

Raw bursty signals.

Tierfull

A bursty signal decorrelates within a fraction of a cycle through bursts that do not travel. Analyse the band-passed oscillation for waves, not the raw value.

Selection by fit quality.

Tierfull

Directions and speeds come only from clean fits, which can be a small minority of agents (2–24% in the motivating model). Report cleanShare next to any direction or speed.

Movie colour scales.

Tierfull

A colour scale that rescales every frame makes each frame's strongest burst look equally strong and exaggerates apparent turbulence. Fix the colour range across frames.
